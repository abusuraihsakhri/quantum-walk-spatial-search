Method

The model evolves the uniform initial state using H = −γA − |w⟩⟨w|, where A is the adjacency matrix of the complete graph and w is its marked vertex. Permutation symmetry reduces the evolution exactly to a two-dimensional invariant subspace. At γ = 1/N, the ideal success probability reaches 1 at time π√N/2. This is an idealized continuous-time model, not a benchmark of a physical device or an arbitrary-graph solver.
